# Acceptance

- `main` remains unchanged; `stable` is the sole integration target.
- Existing uncommitted work is recoverable as an explicitly unverified snapshot branch.
- While busy, Enter and the single primary button follow the stored queue/steer preference; Cmd/Ctrl+Enter selects the other track. An empty busy draft shows Stop. Queued-message flush remains in the queue pill.
- Keep the UI-overhaul decomposition and semantic token system; do not resurrect the obsolete monolithic ChatInput.
- Do not delete the original repository until the selected source, submodule pins, local snapshots and runtime-data retention are independently recoverable and the applicable verification is acceptable.
