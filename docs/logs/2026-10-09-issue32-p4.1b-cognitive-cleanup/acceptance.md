# P4.1b acceptance

## Engineering acceptance

Accepted for local Linux engineering scope. Every tested App composition
failure after cognitive bundle creation releases that owner once, retains both
initialization and cleanup causes, and releases storage after the bundle. A
fresh bundle and storage owner can be created on retry. Successful composition
transfers ownership to App.Close exactly once. Focused and full App tests pass.

consumer. This phase does not claim native Windows installed-product
