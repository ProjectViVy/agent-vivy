## Interfaces and review focus

Consume `NotebookReports({entry,onGenerated})` and existing `ReportResult.generation`. Produce natural first-use controls through existing `ReportsClient.generate` and settings read/write. Reuse the same callback to refresh/select the generation's real destination entry; do not assume it belongs to the currently filtered section. Keep one generation control instance per visible state.

Resolve `result.generation.entry_id` through existing `NotebookClient.getEntry({id})` to obtain its authoritative section before selecting/refreshing it. `GenerationProvenance` has entry_id/revision_id but no section_id; do not infer destination from period or add a duplicate DTO field.

Review empty section, no selected entry with populated list, absent report module, completion after navigation, and generated versus human provenance. A dirty editor must retain its buffer; this change must not silently broaden that contract.
